No app account or payment is required. Open a project folder and grant file access; microphone access is only needed to record. Optional mVocalToolkit processing runs on the user's computer, not a developer-hosted service. Connect both devices to the same network. On the computer: mLabeler > Settings > Autolabel, install the toolkit and enable Let phones connect. On Android: Settings > Autolabel, enter the displayed address and token. No shared username or password exists.
